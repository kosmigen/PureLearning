PureLearning Sans / PureLearning Serif (reading fonts)

- Sources:
  https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans/blob/release/Variable/WOFF2/TTF/Subset/SourceHanSansCN-VF.ttf.woff2
  https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif/blob/release/Variable/WOFF2/TTF/Subset/SourceHanSerifCN-VF.ttf.woff2
- Copyright 2014-2025 Adobe (Sans); Copyright 2017-2024 Adobe (Serif).
- Reserved Font Name: Source. Derived font names are PureLearning Sans
  and PureLearning Serif; original copyright and license remain embedded.
- Complete source character coverage is split into Unicode-ranged WOFF2
  shards, with hinting removed. Weights 250-900 remain variable.
- Rebuild instructions and source hashes: scripts/subset_reading_fonts.py.
- reading-sans/ is used for navigation, headings and technical prose;
  reading-serif/ remains available as an alternative reading font.
