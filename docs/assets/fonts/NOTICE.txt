PureLearning Sans (navigation and technical reading font)
- Derived web subsets of Adobe Source Han Sans CN VF 2.005
  (Simplified Chinese regional glyphs).
- Source:
  https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans/blob/release/Variable/WOFF2/TTF/Subset/SourceHanSansCN-VF.ttf.woff2
- Copyright 2014-2025 Adobe.
- Reserved Font Name: Source. The derived family is named PureLearning Sans;
  original copyright and license remain embedded.
- Complete source character coverage is split into Unicode-ranged WOFF2
  shards, with hinting removed. Weights 250-900 remain variable.
- Rebuild instructions and source hashes: scripts/subset_reading_fonts.py.
- reading-sans/ is used for navigation, headings and technical prose.
